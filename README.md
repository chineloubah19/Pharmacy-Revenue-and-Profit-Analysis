# Pharmacy Revenue and Profit Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project explores pharmacy sales and business performance using Power BI. The goal was to analyse revenue, profitability, product performance, pharmacy performance and sales trends to uncover insights that can support better business decisions.

The interactive dashboard provides an overview of key performance indicators, highlights revenue-generating categories and pharmacies, and examines how different business factors contribute to overall performance.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

- What is the overall revenue and profit performance?
- How does revenue change across months and years?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which pharmacies contribute the most to total revenue?
- How do different pharmacy types perform?
- What proportion of revenue comes from promotional sales?
- How do store sizes contribute to revenue?
- Which brands and products perform best?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of pharmacy business performance.

Key metrics:
- Total Revenue: €8.63M
- Total Profit: €2.42M
- Total Units Sold: 446K
- Profit Margin: 28.04%

Visualizations include:
- Monthly revenue comparison and profit margin trend.
- Revenue and profit by product category.
- Top five pharmacies by revenue.
- Revenue and profit contribution by country.
- Revenue contribution by promotional sales.
- Revenue by pharmacy type.

### 2. Product Performance Dashboard

Focuses on product and brand performance.

Key visualizations:
- Monthly order trend.
- Active versus discontinued products.
- Top 10 brands by total orders.
- Orders by city.
- Product performance table showing revenue, profit, orders and profit margin.

### 3. Pharmacy Performance Dashboard

Examines performance across individual pharmacies and store characteristics.

Key visualizations:
- Top five pharmacies by revenue.
- Bottom five pharmacies by revenue.
- Revenue by store size.
- Pharmacy performance insights table.
- Revenue distribution across pharmacy types.

## Key Business Insights

1. The business generated €8.63M in total revenue and €2.42M in profit, with an overall profit margin of 28.04%.

2. Prescription products emerged as the highest revenue-generating category.

3. Germany contributed 18.2% of total revenue, making it the leading country in the analysis.

4. Promotional transactions accounted for 10.55% of total revenue.

5. Urban pharmacies generated the highest revenue among the three pharmacy types, followed by suburban and rural pharmacies.

6. Revenue performance varies across individual pharmacies, highlighting opportunities to investigate differences in operational and sales performance.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritise high-performing prescription products to sustain revenue growth.
- Investigate underperforming pharmacies to identify operational and sales improvement opportunities.
- Explore growth opportunities in suburban and rural markets.
- Evaluate promotional effectiveness to determine whether promotional activities are delivering sufficient returns.
- Monitor monthly revenue and profit margin trends to identify changes in business performance.
- Use product and brand performance insights to support inventory and sales planning.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI to transform pharmacy sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations, performance indicators and data-driven recommendations.

The dashboard is designed to support strategic decision-making around revenue growth, profitability, product performance and pharmacy operations.
